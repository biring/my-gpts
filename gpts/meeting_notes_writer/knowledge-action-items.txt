PURPOSE
This document defines how decisions and action items are classified, phrased, and reconciled against a prior tracker.

This document supplements the Core GPT Instructions with action-item-specific rules and examples. It does not override the Instructions; where they conflict, the Instructions take precedence.


SCOPE
Applies to:
- The Decisions and Action Items sections of every Generation Mode response.
- Reconciliation of a prior action tracker or previous meeting notes, when provided.

Does not apply to:
- Section order, headings, or chat formatting — see knowledge-output.txt.
- The downloadable .docx report structure — see knowledge-report.txt.


RULES

# Decisions:
- Write each decision as a concise outcome starting with a verb, e.g. "Proceed with...", "Close the item because...", "Prioritize...", "Continue evaluating...", "Complete pre-work before...".
- A routine status update (e.g. "on track", "in progress") is not a decision; do not log it as one unless the transcript states an explicit decision or agreed direction alongside it.

# Action Items:
- Scan the entire transcript for commitments to a future action tied to an owner or clear next step (e.g. "I'll...", "we'll...", "let's schedule..."), including those raised in side discussions unrelated to the meeting's stated agenda; do not limit extraction to the meeting's primary topics.
- Create an action item only when both are true: (1) a specific future deliverable or decision-prep output is requested or committed, not merely discussed or debated; (2) the item is not a subset, example, or supporting detail of a broader action already captured (see merge rule below). If either is false, do not create a standalone action item. Owner is captured separately per the Owner field rule below, including "Unassigned" when no speaker label or explicit name is present.
- Applying test (1): excludes examples used to illustrate a category; comments, opinions, or suggestions offered without a commitment; "we should consider..." statements unless someone is assigned to investigate or decide; historical or background context (e.g. "we usually travel to Singapore"); and general observations or shared information offered with no commitment language.
- Each action starts with a clear verb, names the project or topic, and states the expected deliverable, e.g. "Finalize Q3 budget draft for finance review" rather than "budget draft."
- Owner is stated only when a speaker label, explicit name, or a collectively-named group (e.g. "the team," a named department) is present in the transcript; otherwise "Unassigned."
- Due Date is stated only when explicitly given; otherwise "Not stated." If a single due date is stated as covering multiple requested items collectively (e.g. "get me all of this by Friday"), apply that due date to each of those action items — not only the one nearest the statement.
- Date Created: see Reconciliation below for how it is set.
- Notes: captures any important update, context, or blocker about the item raised in this meeting (e.g. progress, a blocker, why a due date moved). Use "—" when nothing new was discussed.
- Do not combine two separate topics into a single action item.
- Merge rule: before finalizing the table, compare each candidate item against every other candidate item. If one is a subset, example, or supporting/implementation detail of another — such as a specific purchase or record lookup that supports a broader budget request, or a specific example offered under a broader request for inputs — merge the narrower item into the broader item's Notes field instead of listing it as a separate row. This differs from "do not combine two separate topics into a single action item," which forbids collapsing unrelated topics into one row — merging a subset into Notes keeps one broader action, not two combined ones.
- Aggressive action rollup rule: After identifying candidate action items, perform a second-pass consolidation by deliverable/outcome. If multiple candidate actions are required only to complete the same broader deliverable, merge them into one action item and place the sub-actions in Notes. Do not keep separate rows merely because the sub-actions have different sources, files, tracker fields, data owners, or implementation steps. Keep actions separate only when they produce distinct deliverables, require different decision forums, have materially different due dates, or can be completed independently without serving the same outcome.
- Preferred action granularity: Action items should be captured at the level a manager would review in a follow-up meeting, not at the level of every implementation step discussed in the transcript. Target the smallest number of rows that still preserves distinct deliverables, owners, due dates, risks, and decisions.
- Do not track minor administrative tasks (e.g. scheduling a follow-up invite) as standard items unless they affect project delivery; instead exclude them (see Materiality below).

# Reconciliation (when a prior tracker or previous meeting notes is provided):
- Review every open item in the prior tracker before adding new items from this meeting.
- Every open prior item appears in this meeting's Action Items table, assigned exactly one Status: Updated or Carried Forward (or Closed, if resolved this meeting).
- Date Created for a carried-forward, updated, or closed item is preserved from the prior tracker if it states one; if the prior tracker does not state a creation date, use "Not stated" — never infer it.
- Carried-forward or updated items may be reworded for clarity, or enriched with new context learned in this meeting (captured in Notes), but original intent must be preserved — never replace intent.
- New items identified in this meeting are added after reconciled items, with Status New and Date Created set to this meeting's Date (from Meeting Info); if the meeting Date is not stated, use "Not stated."
- An item already marked Closed in the prior tracker is not carried forward or re-listed in this meeting's Action Items table — only items still open (New, Carried Forward, or Updated) in the prior tracker are reconciled.

# Materiality flag:
- An action item judged administratively minor is excluded from the Action Items table; list it instead as a bullet in Assumptions & Exclusions (see knowledge-output.txt), prefixed "[Minor]" — never dropped silently.
- The user may ask, after generation, to add a minor item to the Action Items table, or to remove/merge any standard action item.
- This exclusion applies only to items newly identified in this meeting. A prior-tracker (reconciled) item always remains in the Action Items table regardless of materiality — see Reconciliation and its Forbidden Practices rule against omission. If judged minor, keep it in the table with the "[Minor]" prefix rather than excluding it.

# Action item Status:
Allowed values: New, Carried Forward, Updated, Closed.
- New: first appears in this meeting; not in the prior tracker.
- Carried Forward: in the prior tracker; not discussed as resolved or changed this meeting.
- Updated: in the prior tracker; owner, action, or due date changed based on this meeting.
- Closed: in the prior tracker; explicitly stated as done or resolved in this meeting's transcript.


FORBIDDEN PRACTICES
- Do not log a routine status update as a Decision.
- Do not invent a Due Date, Owner, Date Created, or Status not supported by the transcript or prior tracker.
- Do not mark an item Closed unless the transcript explicitly states it is done or resolved.
- Do not drop a minor action item silently — list it under Assumptions & Exclusions with "[Minor]" prefixed instead of the Action Items table.
- Do not omit any open prior tracker item from the Action Items table (already-Closed items are excluded — see Reconciliation).
- Do not leave Notes blank when the transcript states something important about the item — use "—" only when nothing new was discussed.
- Do not apply a collectively-stated due date to only one of the items it covers.
- Do not list a subset, example, or supporting-detail item as a separate action row once the merge rule applies — fold it into the broader item's Notes instead.


DECISION CRITERIA FOR AMBIGUOUS CASES
- If it is unclear whether a prior item was resolved this meeting, mark it Carried Forward, not Closed.
- If it is unclear whether a statement is a genuine commitment or just discussion, do not log it as an action item.
- If it is unclear whether an item is a distinct action or a subset of a broader one already captured, default to merging it into the broader action's Notes.
- If it is unclear whether an action item is administratively minor or delivery-affecting, default to treating it as minor and list it in Assumptions & Exclusions.
- When an item is defaulted to Carried Forward under these criteria, record this in Assumptions & Exclusions (see knowledge-output.txt). An item defaulted to minor is already recorded there per the Materiality flag rule — no separate note needed.


EXAMPLE 1
Prior tracker included: "Owner: Mark Ito | Action: Draft Q3 budget | Due: 2026-07-30 | Created: 2026-07-10 | Status: Open".
This meeting's transcript states: "Mark said the Q3 budget draft is done but finance still needs to review it by August 5."

Resulting Action Items row:
# 1 | Finalize Q3 budget draft for finance review | Mark Ito | 2026-08-05 | 2026-07-10 | Updated | Draft complete; awaiting finance sign-off.

Resulting Decision (if the transcript states it explicitly): "Proceed with finance review of the Q3 budget draft."


EXAMPLE 2
Meeting Date: 2026-07-31.
Transcript states: "Bhavdeep asked the team to provide engineering development budget needs for prototype parts and unfunded work. Tom said his team buys items regularly and he can look up approved POs to show recent spend."

The PO lookup is supporting detail for the broader budget-input request, not a separate deliverable — merge it into that action's Notes rather than listing it separately.

Resulting Action Items row (one row, not two):
# 1 | Provide engineering development budget needs for prototype parts, board spins, and unfunded work | Team | Not stated | 2026-07-31 | New | Tom said he can look up approved POs/purchases from his team to inform the estimate.


CHECKLIST
Before finalizing, confirm:
- Every distinct topic or side discussion was checked for a committed next step, not only the main agenda items.
- Every Decision is phrased as a verb-led outcome; no routine status update appears as a Decision.
- Every Action Item starts with a verb, names the topic, and states the deliverable.
- A due date covering multiple items was applied to each of them.
- Every open prior tracker item is present with Status Updated or Carried Forward (or Closed, if resolved this meeting), and its original Date Created preserved (or "Not stated" if the tracker didn't state one).
- New items from this meeting are listed with Status New and Date Created set to the meeting Date.
- Minor items appear in Assumptions & Exclusions, prefixed "[Minor]", not in the Action Items table.
- Notes is "—" only when nothing new was discussed about the item this meeting.
- No Due Date, Owner, Date Created, or Status was invented beyond what the transcript or prior tracker supports.
- No action item was logged from general discussion without an explicit commitment.
- Every candidate action item was checked against every other candidate for a subset/example/supporting-detail relationship, and merged into the broader item's Notes when found.
- Perform a final action-rollup pass: ask whether rows would be reviewed separately in the next meeting or are sub-steps of the same update. Merge rows that would be reviewed together into broader outcome-level action items, with sub-actions retained in Notes.
